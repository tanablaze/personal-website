MY JOURNEY

My Story

My name is Unity Santana Sunday. My journey has been shaped by learning, service, leadership, and the courage to keep growing through different seasons of life.

A Different Beginning

In 2014, I completed my National Diploma in Maritime Safety and Transport Management at Global Maritime Academy in Nigeria. I chose this field because I wanted to explore a path that was unconventional and one where fewer women ventured.

Growing Through Change

Over time, my interests expanded toward technology, software development, project management, and leadership. While balancing work and study, I reconsidered how best to pursue these interests and continued my education through BYU-Pathway Worldwide.

I am pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Professional Studies, with an expected completion date of April 2027, subject to my official academic record. This journey is helping me connect technical learning with practical operations, communication, and leadership.

What I Have Learned

My journey has taught me that changing direction does not mean that earlier decisions were wasted. Every experience can contribute to the person we are becoming. I continue to learn, serve others, and build skills that can make a meaningful difference.
