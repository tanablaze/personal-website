MY STORY

The Courage to Choose Differently

My educational journey began with a desire to explore a field that was different from the conventional paths around me. I chose Maritime Safety and Transport Management because I wanted to venture into an area where fewer women were represented.

After completing my National Diploma, my desire to learn and grow did not end. Years later, I returned to education with an interest in technology and initially hoped to pursue Software Engineering.

While balancing work, study and other responsibilities, I received guidance from my academic mentor to consider Professional Studies. I made the adjustment while continuing to pursue my broader interests in technology, project management, business and leadership.

I do not view this journey as a reason for regret. I see it as a reminder that a meaningful path can involve adjustment, learning and decisions that respond to life's circumstances.

"I may not have followed the exact path I first imagined, but I am still moving forward with purpose. And I do not regret the journey that has brought me here."

This website is one way I am documenting that journey and creating room for what comes next.
