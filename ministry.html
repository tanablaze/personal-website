FAITH, SERVICE AND LEADERSHIP

My Ministry

Ministry is an important part of my life. It gives me opportunities to serve people, support their growth, and contribute to the work of my church community.

Pastoral Service

I serve in ministry as a Teens and Youth Pastor, supporting young people as they learn, grow, and develop in their faith.

Operations and Administration

My responsibilities have also included operations and administration at Christ Embassy. This experience connects service with organisation, communication, coordination, and leadership.

Serving Through Leadership

I believe leadership involves more than holding a title. It also means being dependable, communicating clearly, helping people work together, and being willing to learn.

My Approach

I want my service to reflect compassion, integrity, humility, and a genuine commitment to helping others. I continue to learn how to become a more effective leader in both ministry and professional settings.
